# Tsugi

*Tsugi (次) means "next".* Tsugi is an Android app that recommends anime based on what you've watched and how you rated it.

- Tell it what you've seen with a quick poster quiz, or just pick some genres
- Get personal picks, each with a reason ("Because you liked …")
- Browse ready-made collections, sorted by how well each title matches you

No account needed, and your watch history stays on your phone.

Coming soon to Google Play.
