# Tsugi – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 16 October 2026

Tsugi is an anime recommendation app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: **Tsugi has no accounts, collects no personal data, and keeps your watch history on your phone.**

## What Tsugi stores, and where

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device:

- the anime you've rated, your ratings, and anything you marked as dropped
- the genres you picked during setup
- your "Save for later" and "Not interested" lists
- the suggestions the app generated for you
- a temporary copy (cache) of anime information downloaded from AniList, so the app is faster and works offline

Tsugi does not have its own servers. We (the developer) never receive, see or store this information.

## What leaves your device

To show anime and make suggestions, Tsugi requests public anime information from **AniList** (anilist.co), a free anime database. These requests can include:

- what you type into search
- AniList IDs of anime (for example, to fetch details of a title)
- genres and themes used to find candidate suggestions

These requests are not linked to you: there is no account, login, advertising ID or device ID attached. Like any website, AniList's servers see your device's IP address when it connects. Poster images are loaded directly from AniList's image servers in the same way. AniList's handling of this is covered by [AniList's own terms and policies](https://anilist.co/terms).

Tsugi is not affiliated with or endorsed by AniList.

## What Tsugi does not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising, and no advertising IDs
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone
- No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files

The only permission Tsugi uses is **internet access**, to reach AniList.

## Backups

If your phone's Android backup (for example to your Google account) is switched on, Android may include Tsugi's data in that backup and restore it when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google, not by Tsugi, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

## Deleting your data

- To change or remove individual entries, use **My Anime** in the app.
- To delete everything, uninstall Tsugi or go to **Settings → Apps → Tsugi → Storage → Clear data** on your phone.

Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children

Tsugi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone. Adult-rated titles are filtered out of the app.

## Future changes

If Tsugi adds features that change any of the above (for example an optional in-app purchase through Google Play), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the effective date above will change. Purchases through Google Play are handled by Google under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
